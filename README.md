# ⚡ UrjaSaathi – AI-Based Energy Optimization Platform

## 📌 Overview
UrjaSaathi is an AI-powered system designed to help households track and optimize electricity consumption at the appliance level.

## 🎯 Problem
Most households do not know which appliances consume the most electricity, leading to high bills and energy waste.

## 💡 Solution
UrjaSaathi provides:
- Appliance-wise energy tracking
- Personalized saving recommendations
- Smart insights using AI

## 🛠️ Features
- Real-time energy monitoring
- AI-based consumption analysis
- Cost-saving recommendations
- Multilingual support (English, Hindi)

## 📊 Market Opportunity
- 250+ million households in India
- Rising electricity costs
- Growing smart home market

## 💰 Business Model
- Freemium mobile app
- Premium analytics subscription
- Partnership with solar providers
